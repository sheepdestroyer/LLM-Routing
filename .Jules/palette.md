## 2026-06-15 - Decorative Emoji Accessibility
**Learning:** Jinja2 templates frequently embed emojis directly within text nodes or loosely structured spans (like `<span>⚠️</span>`). This causes screen readers to loudly announce the literal emoji names ("warning sign", "check mark", "brain"), muddying the auditory experience of dashboard statuses.
**Action:** Always wrap purely decorative emojis in `<span aria-hidden="true">` to silence them for screen reader users, while preserving their visual impact. When writing Python patch scripts to automate this, target specific emoji characters and carefully replace spans to ensure the `aria-hidden` attribute is injected cleanly without breaking template syntax.
